---
title: Multi-currency
description: Sell one plan in several currencies, and re-price it for specific countries.
---

A plan has one price per currency you sell it in. There is no base currency: USD, EUR and NPR prices of the same plan are equal peers.

```
Plan "Pro"
  ├── USD  29.00
  ├── EUR  27.00
  └── NPR  3,900.00
```

Prices are in the currency's normal unit, not in cents: `29` means $29.00.

## The currency is locked at subscription

When a customer subscribes, the subscription takes one currency and keeps it for life. Renewals, seat changes and add-on purchases are all charged in that currency.

## How checkout picks the currency

1. The `currency` you pass to checkout, if the plan has a price in it.
2. Otherwise, the plan's first currency.

A currency the plan has no price for is refused with `422 CURRENCY_NOT_SUPPORTED`.

## Per-seat plans

A per-seat plan has a base price and a per-seat price in each currency:

| Currency | Base price | Per extra seat |
|---|---|---|
| USD | 50.00 | 5.00 |
| NPR | 6,000.00 | 600.00 |

## Country prices

A plan can also have a different price for buyers in a specific country, in one of its currencies. For example, Pro can cost 19.00 USD for buyers in India and 29.00 USD elsewhere.

A customer gets the country price when their country is set on the customer and the plan has a price for that country in the checkout currency. The country is fixed on the subscription when it is created.

<Note>
  `GET /api/public/plans` and the hosted pricing page show the plan's regular price. A customer with a country price is charged the country price at checkout.
</Note>

## Add-ons

Add-ons are bought in the subscription's currency, so give each add-on a price in every currency your plans use. A plan can override an add-on's price per currency. See [Add-ons](/concepts/addons#prices).

## Payment providers by currency

| Currency | Providers |
|---|---|
| USD, EUR and other card currencies | Stripe |
| NPR | ConnectIPS |

## Setting prices

Set a plan's currencies and country prices in its **Set price** flow, under **Product catalog**. Set an add-on's prices on the add-on, and a plan's overrides on the plan's **Compatible add-ons** card.
