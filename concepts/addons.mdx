---
title: Add-ons
description: Extra credits a customer buys on top of their plan.
---

An **add-on** is something a customer buys on top of their subscription, such as an "AI Token Pack" that adds 1,000 `ai_tokens`. Use add-ons to let customers top up a metered resource without changing plan.

## How add-ons work

1. Create the add-on in your product under **Product catalog → Catalog → Add-ons**. An add-on belongs to one product.
2. Give it an **entitlement**: the meter it tops up and how much it grants.
3. Set its price in each currency you sell in.
4. Make it **compatible** with the plans that may buy it. A customer only sees add-ons compatible with their current plan.
5. Publish it.

## Fixed fee or per unit

| Pricing | The customer buys | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed fee** | A fixed pack for one price | 1,000 tokens for $15 |
| **Per unit bought** | Any quantity, priced per unit | $0.02 per token |

## Expiry

| Setting | The add-on's credits expire |
|---|---|
| Ends with the billing period | At the end of the customer's current period |
| Expires after a number of days | That many days after purchase |
| Never expires | Never |

## Prices

An add-on is bought in the currency of the customer's subscription, so give it a price in every currency your plans use.

A plan can **override** an add-on's price. For example, Pro customers can pay $10 for a pack that costs $15 elsewhere. Customers on that plan are charged the override.

A price of 0 is allowed. A free add-on calls no payment provider: its credits are granted at once, and the purchase returns `requiresPayment: false` with no `checkoutUrl`.

## What cannot be published yet

The public API describes an add-on as one meter and one quantity. Until it can describe more, two kinds of add-on stay in draft:

- an add-on that grants a **feature** instead of credits
- an add-on that tops up **more than one meter**

The dashboard explains the refusal when you try to publish one.

## Selling add-ons from the dashboard

From a subscription in the dashboard, **Buy add-on** invoices the customer for an add-on their plan supports. The credits are granted when the invoice is paid. A free add-on is granted at once.

## Selling add-ons in your app

Customers can buy add-ons on their billing page. To sell them inside your own app, use the [add-ons guide](/guides/addons).
