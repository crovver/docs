---
title: Plans
description: What a customer pays and what they get, inside one product.
---

A **plan** belongs to one [product](/concepts/products). It has a **price**, which says what the customer pays, and **entitlements**, which say what the customer gets. You build both in the dashboard, under **Product catalog**, with the plan's **Set price** flow.

## Plan types

| Type | What the customer pays | Payment provider |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Nothing | None. The subscription activates at once. |
| **Flat Rate** | A fixed price every billing cycle | Required |
| **Per Seat** | A base price that covers a number of seats, plus a price for each extra seat | Required |

A per-seat plan counts seats with a **provisioned meter**, such as `seats`, that you pick in the flow. Its price is:

```
total = base price + max(0, seats − included seats) × price per extra seat
```

For example, a base of $50 that includes 5 seats, plus $10 per extra seat, costs $80 for 8 seats.

A per-seat plan can instead be priced in **tiers**: bands of seats, each with one flat price for the whole period. The price is the band that holds the seat count, not seats × a rate:

| Band | Price per period |
|---|---|
| 1–10 seats | $80 |
| 11–50 seats | $250 |
| 51+ seats | $400 |

So 11 seats cost $250, and 40 seats cost $250 too. The bands must start at 1 and end with an open top band. The plans API lists them in `seatTiers`, and a tiered plan's `basePrice` and `perSeatPrice` are 0. See [Seat-based billing](/guides/seat-based-billing#tiered-seat-pricing).

## Billing period, trial and currencies

- **Billing period**: monthly or annual. The flow also lets you list extra periods with a discount. Checkout and renewals currently charge the plan's main period only.
- **Free trial**: a number of days before the first charge. A subscription in trial has full access.
- **Currencies**: give the plan a price in each currency you sell in. You can also re-price it for buyers in specific countries. See [Multi-currency](/concepts/multi-currency).

## Entitlements

Entitlements are what a plan grants. There are two kinds:

**Features** are on/off. A plan either grants `advanced-reporting` or it doesn't. Your app checks them with [`canAccess`](/concepts/entitlements).

**Meter allowances** give the customer a quantity of a metered meter each billing period, such as 10,000 `api_calls`. Each allowance has an enforcement setting:

| Enforcement | When the allowance runs out |
|---|---|
| **Block** | Further consumption is refused with `blocked` |
| **Track** | Consumption still succeeds and returns `warning`, so you can alert or bill for it yourself |

Allowances reset to the full amount at the start of every billing period. Unused credits do not carry over. See [Credits](/concepts/credits).

## Feature and meter keys

The key is what your code passes to Crovver, so it must match exactly.

| Kind | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Feature | lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens | `advanced-reporting` |
| Meter | lowercase letters, digits and single underscores | `api_calls` |

A key cannot be changed after it is created. Plans created before the current dashboard may use older keys, and those keep working exactly as they are.

## Visibility

A plan can be hidden from customers. A hidden plan is not listed on the pricing page or by `GET /api/public/plans`. Use it for deals you set up by hand from the dashboard.

## Changing a plan that has subscribers

Once a plan has subscribers, its pricing is locked, so nobody is silently charged a new price. To change the price, create a new plan, publish it and archive the old one. Existing subscribers stay on the old plan and keep renewing.

## Lifecycle

Plans are drafts until published, and only a published plan of a published product is sold. See [Products](/concepts/products#lifecycle-draft-published-archived).
