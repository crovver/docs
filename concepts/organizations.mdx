---
title: Organizations
description: Your company's account in Crovver, which owns everything else.
---

An **organization** is the account you create when you sign up for Crovver. It represents your company. It owns your products and plans, your payment provider connections, your API keys, your team and your customers.

## What belongs to an organization

```
Organization
├── Products               ← what you sell, each B2B or D2C
│   └── Plans              ← prices and entitlements
├── Features and meters    ← what plans grant
├── Add-ons                ← extras sold on top of a plan
├── Payment providers      ← Stripe, ConnectIPS
├── API keys and webhooks  ← how your app talks to Crovver
├── Team                   ← people who use the dashboard
└── Customers              ← your customers
    └── Subscriptions      ← what each customer pays for
```

## B2B or D2C is set per product

Whether your customers are companies or individuals is a property of each **product**, not of the organization. One organization can sell a B2B product and a D2C product side by side. See [Products](/concepts/products).

## Your team

Invite teammates under **Settings → Team**. Each member has a role that decides what they can see and change in the dashboard. A member can belong to more than one organization and switch between them from the sidebar.

## API keys

Every organization has two kinds of key:

| Key | Prefix | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Public key | `pk_live_` | Your frontend, through the React SDK |
| Secret key | `sk_live_` | Your backend only |

Crovver shows both keys once, when they are created. Manage them under **Developers → API Keys**. See [Authentication](/authentication/overview).

## Your Crovver subscription

Your organization subscribes to Crovver itself, the same way your customers subscribe to you. The dashboard asks you to choose a Crovver plan before it opens, and you manage it from **Settings → Billing**.
