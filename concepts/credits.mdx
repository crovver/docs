---
title: Credits
description: How metered allowances are granted, consumed and refilled for each customer.
---

**Credits** are how Crovver meters usage. When a plan gives a [meter allowance](/concepts/plans#entitlements), such as 10,000 `api_calls` a month, each subscriber gets that many credits in a **pool** keyed by the meter. Your backend consumes credits as the customer uses the resource.

## Where credits come from

```
Balance = plan allowance + add-on credits
```

| Source | Granted | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan allowance** | When the subscription activates, then at the start of every billing period | At the end of the period |
| **Add-on credits** | When an [add-on](/concepts/addons) purchase is paid, or at once if it is free | As the add-on says: end of period, after a number of days, or never |

## Refills

Allowances reset at the start of every billing period: the customer gets the full allowance again, and unused plan credits are dropped. Add-on credits are not touched by a refill.

A subscription paid for several periods in advance, such as a monthly plan paid a year ahead, still refills once a month. Crovver refills each period on its own schedule, so the customer never gets the whole year's credits at once.

<Note>
  Pools created before the current dashboard may be set to **roll over** unused credits, up to a cap. They keep working that way. New allowances always reset.
</Note>

## Consuming credits

Call [`POST /api/public/credits/consume`](/api-reference/credits/consume) from your backend with a secret key:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://app.crovver.com/api/public/credits/consume" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "tenantId": "workspace_123",
    "poolKey": "ai_tokens",
    "amount": 50,
    "idempotencyKey": "gen-8f2c1e"
  }'
```

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `allowed` | Credits were deducted |
| `warning` | Credits were deducted, but the pool tracks usage only and is now over its allowance |
| `blocked` | Not enough credits on a pool that blocks usage. Nothing was deducted, and `remaining` says what is left |

The plan allowance is used first, then add-on credits, starting with the ones that expire soonest.

Every call needs an `idempotencyKey`. Sending the same key again returns the first result and deducts nothing, so retries are safe. Derive the key from the action, such as a request or job ID, never from the current time.

## Checking the balance

```bash
GET /api/public/credits/balance?tenantId=workspace_123
Authorization: Bearer sk_live_...
```

The response lists each pool with its plan credits, add-on credits, total and limit. See [Get credit balance](/api-reference/credits/balance).

## More than one product

Credits belong to one subscription. If a customer holds subscriptions to two products, pass `productSlug` on balance and consume calls to choose the product. Without it, Crovver uses the customer's most recent live subscription.

## Canceled subscriptions

A canceled subscription that is still inside its paid period keeps its credits until the period ends. After that, consumption returns `402 NO_SUBSCRIPTION`.

<Warning>
  `record-usage` and `check-usage-limit` are deprecated. `record-usage` writes to a table nothing reads, so usage recorded through it is never counted. Use `credits/consume` and `credits/balance` instead.
</Warning>
